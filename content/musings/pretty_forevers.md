The beast kept looking at the rose, hoping it never sheds. But one by one, almost all went down. “Almost”. But the princess in a shining personality sweeped in and saved the day. Or rather, the story.

Pretty endings are beautiful and moreover possible only in stories. The beast became a beast because of his wrong deeds. Bulbbul killed them all one by one, carrying Her blessings because they were criminals. So supposedly She was keeping a track of the right and wrong. But, does She, really keep a track? Or it’s just something we are told to fool our hearts, like an Aal Izz Well. And stupidly it does believe. Until reality hits and we are forced to face the consequences.

There are no saviour princesses or goddess in reality. It just happens, blurring any lines of right and wrong, because probably there were no lines in the first place. And the only truth that prevails is, you too, shall go one day. It won’t matter if you really finished writing that paper, or wore that dress you had planned, or won that college election or got that grade. But probably it will matter that within how many hearts you ruled, how many couldn’t let you go, and how many are going with you on your last ride. Probably that’s what matters. Probably that, is the princess in the shining personality for our future eternity, and all that we lived for.
