The air conditioner had broken down again. Mid of May, no air conditioner, and a class of 70 students- well not a good combination especially when it’s Kolkata, of all places. But that didn’t deter the professor. He went on chalking down sums in the blackboard, expecting the students to keep copying. Yes, he was on of those professors who didn’t use was projector- kept it the old fashioned way. But again, it was already the third class of the day, still twenty minutes to go for recess. Students were hungry, tired and restless due to the heat. Who can concentrate in such a situation! Some of the students kept clicking pictures of the blackboard- might take down the sums later. The rest complained: Sir please let’s end the class today. The heat is too much, we can’t focus. Even the fans run slow.
Some just stopped paying attention simply : I had my breakfast at 8.30 am. It’s 12.30 pm now, how are we supposed to concentrate in hunger?
The recess bell rang after 10 minutes, the professors left and students left for the cafeteria.
As usual it was packed. Groups of students sat, either with home cooked meal, or bought from the cafeteria. The queue at the billing counter kept increasing, with the staff shouting orders: ” A cappuccino with chocolate biscuits”, “A chicken pizza”, “A spring roll with sauce”…. Recess lasts around 25 minutes. Soon the bell rang, and the crowd dispersed, leaving behind ordered bills, empty cups and paper plates to be cleaned by the staff later. Again back to hectic classes, wishing the rooms are cool and air conditioner won’t break down again.
The classes ended at 4 and students either flocked to the cafeteria or left. That was majorly selfie and instagram time: posting stories about a boiling day with no a.c., or simply bitching about the fact that fees are paid on time and classrooms should be well maintained.

Far from the campus, is a small slum, on the outskirts of the city. At 12 noon, it’s the bath hour for the kids. Two brothers, about 7, runs back home to their mum. They just got back from school. Their school is right next to their home, a small shade with asbestos walls and a sheet of plastic as the roof. Someone once gifted them a blackboard, the teacher writes on that using small pieces of red brick. The students take turn to write on the board eagerly, because they know that writing strengthens memory.
The brothers are really happy today. After one full week, they got the chance to write on the blackboard. They both did a two digit addition, and got it right! The teacher promised to give them a chance again very soon. This made their day. Now it’s bath time and mom will take them to the tap on the other side of the road- it has water once a day from 12 to 12.30. Given the heat of thr may, the water is quite bearable- won’t leave blisters. But they wouldn’t mind today.
A good bath, and both are back to their hut with mum. They feel something in their tummy, mum calls it hunger, but that makes them feel okay. Like everything is normal. They don’t remember what it feels like not to have that tug in the tummy. Like they don’t remember last time they had a full meal, though last Saturday, that is 2 days back from now, their mum bought a potato and boiled it fot them to share. It was delicious, they remember. Yesterday mum got no food, probably she couldn’t manage to get any money. Their neighbor offered them a brinjal but mum refused- everyone has to eat right! Probably today will be another day without any potato or brinjal. But they really don’t care today. They have got two sums correct! Tomorrow they will learn multiplication, though they have heard that by 7, kids already know that. Right now, they have to go out to sell the flowers mum collected in the morning. Then maybe tomorrow will be a potato day! But the heat makes it a little difficult. Still life has to go on. They know complaining won’t get them anywhere. Also, by evening, sun will be gone and it’ll be all good.
But they are too happy today to mind anything at all!
