### Key Investigations
* **Kinematic Core Design:** Developed a Python-based computational tool to simulate and visualize kinematic curves for low-energy, two-body nuclear reactions of the form \\(A(a,b)B\\).
* **Conservation Laws Implementation:** Leveraged fundamental energy and momentum conservation laws, incorporating reaction \\(Q\\)-values and non-relativistic approximations to predict exact energy-angle correlations of secondary reaction products.
* **Experimental Parameters Mapping:** Enabled user-defined parameters for all target, projectile, and detector system properties to aid in pre-experimental design and kinematic validation.

---

### Key Simulation Features
* **Stochastic Vertexing:** Simulates the exact reaction position and resulting angular distributions using Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling arrays.
* **Energy Loss Tracking:** Incorporates deterministic energy-loss algorithms tracking particle degradation inside both the target material and active detector media.
* **Multi-Reaction Capabilities:** Features a scalable framework capable of overlaying multiple concurrent reactions simultaneously.
