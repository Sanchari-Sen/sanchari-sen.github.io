### Major Tasks & Methodology
* **Particle-Matter Modeling:** Simulated full particle-matter interactions in low-to-intermediate energy nuclear reactions using advanced Monte Carlo frameworks.
* **Geometry Configuration:** Hand-coded complex, multi-stage detector geometries based on specific exotic nuclei configurations to accurately track physical energy deposition layers.
* **Data Synthesis Frameworks:** Utilized specialized computational toolkits including **NPTools** and **GEANT4** to build complete detector environments, tracking simulated reaction outputs for deep analytical evaluation.

![det](/images/projects/o14det.png)

---

### Analysis & Optimization
* Deployed the **CERN-ROOT** C++ framework to extract, parse, and analyze heavy output simulation trees.
* Compared synthetic data profiles against established literature baselines to iteratively optimize detector efficiencies.
* Extracted structural metrics including total reaction cross-sections, optimal input beam energies, and simulated particle tracking counts.
